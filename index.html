coach-daily

A personal endurance-training assistant.

coach-daily is a small, single-user program that helps one athlete train for triathlon. Each morning it reads that athlete's own sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and training-load data, scores it against their personal baselines, and sends a short recommendation about the day's session — proceed as planned, modify it, or rest.

What it does with Google Calendar

With the owner's permission, coach-daily writes that week's planned training sessions into their own Google Calendar, and updates those events when the morning recommendation changes — for example retitling a session and noting why it was modified.

  • It only ever reads and writes events it created.
  • It acts solely on the calendar of the person who authorized it.
  • It does not share calendar data with anyone.

Who it's for

It is a personal project, not a product or a service. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no users other than its author.

Not medical advice

coach-daily produces training suggestions from consumer wearable data. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Anyone with pain, illness or health concerns should consult a qualified professional.
