Privacy policy

coach-daily · last updated 16 November 2026

coach-daily is a personal, single-user program. Its only user is its author, who is also the only person whose data it handles.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar events, through the calendar.events scope, to create and update training sessions on the calendar of the person who authorized it.
  • Training and recovery data from that person's own connected sports watch account — sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts and training load.
  • Self-reported notes that person sends it, such as soreness and sleep quality.

What it does with it

The data is used only to produce training recommendations for that same person, and to write their planned sessions to their own calendar. It is stored in a private code repository that only its author can read.

What it does not do

  • It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.
  • It is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • It is not used to train machine-learning models.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than its author.
  • It reads no calendar events other than the ones it created.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the private repository until its author deletes it, which they can do at any time by deleting the repository. Google account access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all calendar access.

Limited use

coach-daily's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the project's author through the contact address registered on its Google Cloud project.
